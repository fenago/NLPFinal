# Usability Heuristic Review (S56)

**Owner:** `ux-designer-apple-hig` · **Shared with:** —

Heuristics: visibility of system status · match with the real world · user control & freedom · consistency & standards · error prevention · recognition over recall · flexibility & efficiency · aesthetic & minimalist design · help users recover from errors · help & documentation.
Process:
1. Walk the key flows: first visit → understand dashboard; drill into a review; analyze your own review (cold start); learn what NLTK vs spaCy means.
2. Log each problem: where, heuristic, evidence, who is affected.
3. Severity 0–4 (cosmetic → catastrophe) × frequency → rank.
4. One concrete fix each, owner agent, effort S/M/L.
Output a ranked table, top problems first.

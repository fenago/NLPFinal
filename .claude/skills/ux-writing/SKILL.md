# UX Writing (S34)

**Owner:** `frontend-engineer` · **Shared with:** `technical-writer`, `dataviz-specialist`

- Plain, short, active voice. Buttons are verbs ("Analyze review").
- Explain the live limitation once, clearly: "Live analysis uses NLTK in your browser. People, places, brands and products (spaCy) are shown for dataset reviews and examples."
- Errors say what happened and what to do next.
- Empty states suggest an example review to try.
